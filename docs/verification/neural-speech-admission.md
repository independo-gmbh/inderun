# Neural speech admission record

Which neural speech models IndeRun may ship, demonstrate or recommend, and on what terms. One row per asset in
[`neural-model-catalog.json`](../../contracts/fixtures/speech/neural-model-catalog.json), plus the two parts that are
not catalog entries: the German text frontend and the runtime. It records what the catalog says and nothing more. Where
the catalog states no admission, this document does not either. The measured speed of the same models is in
[neural-speech.md](./neural-speech.md); the milestone work is tracked in
[#251](https://github.com/independo-gmbh/inderun/issues/251) and
[#252](https://github.com/independo-gmbh/inderun/issues/252).

## Status

**Nothing here is admitted for production yet.** The int8 Articulino entry is `benchmark_only` and its notes say legal
sign-off is pending (#260). The Core AI entry is `manual_install`: no `.aimodel` is published, so an app installs it by
hand. The demo voices carry a custom licence for demo and evaluation use, and their legal sign-off is pending as well.
`review.status` and its notes are copied from the catalog; a change there is the only way a row's status changes.

## Reading the flags

- **GPL / NC / OpenRAIL.** The catalog declares none of them for any asset: the Articulino weights are Apache-2.0 with
  `restrictions: none`. That is the catalog's statement, not an independent legal review. The voices are the exception
  that matters: `LicenseRef-Independo-Demo-Voices` is a custom licence for voices of consented
  speakers: the catalog limits them to demo and evaluation use.
- **Attribution.** The weights' licence entry carries an attribution text that must travel with them. It names the model
  (Copyright 2026 eigenhand, Apache License 2.0) and the datasets the upstream model was trained on. Three of those are
  CC BY 4.0 (Emilia-YODAS German subset, Hi-Fi TTS 2 selected English chapters, CML-TTS German), which require attribution
  of their own. The upstream `NOTICE` is part of the obligation. See the `license.attribution` field of the entry for the
  exact text.
- **Derived file.** `text.onnx` is not the upstream file: it is rebuilt with an int64 mask. It is listed under the
  weights' licence because the catalog gives the whole entry one licence, and it is hosted on this repository's release
  `neural-speech-assets-1`.
- **Runtime and frontend.** ONNX Runtime and the in-repo normalizer are MIT and have no catalog row, so they have no
  `review.status`.
- **Families beyond Articulino.** Supertonic 3 is pending (#277); Kokoro, Piper and Pocket TTS are deferred (see #277).
  None has a catalog entry, so none is admitted.

## Update rule

Change a row by changing the catalog, not this document. When a legal sign-off lands, the catalog entry's `review`
changes in the same commit that removes the "pending" wording here.
